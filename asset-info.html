📚 Asset Class Information

Understand the 6 asset classes in the game
🇺🇸

US EQ

📈 Equity

US Equities represent shares of publicly traded companies in the United States. They are the largest and most liquid equity market globally.

Characteristics: High growth potential, higher volatility, influenced by US economic data, corporate earnings, and Federal Reserve policy.
🇪🇺

Europe EQ

📈 Equity

European Equities include companies from developed European markets like the UK, Germany, France, and Switzerland.

Characteristics: More cyclical than US equities, influenced by European Central Bank policy, political developments, and global trade.
🇨🇳

China EQ

📈 Equity

Chinese Equities represent shares of companies listed in mainland China and Hong Kong, spanning technology, consumer, and industrial sectors.

Characteristics: High growth potential, higher volatility, influenced by government policy, regulatory changes, and global trade relations.
🌍

Global Bonds

📊 Bond

Global Bonds are fixed-income securities issued by governments and corporations around the world. They provide income and stability.

Characteristics: Lower volatility than equities, interest rate sensitive, provides income, safe-haven during market stress.
🥇

Gold

🏆 Commodity

Gold is a precious metal that serves as a store of value and hedge against inflation, currency devaluation, and geopolitical uncertainty.

Characteristics: Safe-haven asset, inflation hedge, uncorrelated to stocks and bonds, performs well during crises.
💵

Cash

💰 Cash

Cash represents holding money in short-term, low-risk instruments like money market funds, treasury bills, or bank deposits.

Characteristics: Lowest risk, lowest return, provides liquidity, preserves capital during market downturns.

📋 Asset Type Legend

📈 Equity — Ownership in companies, high growth potential
📊 Bond — Fixed income, lower volatility
🏆 Commodity — Physical assets, inflation hedge
💰 Cash — Lowest risk, preserves capital
Understanding asset classes helps you make better investment decisions
